StuPivot is designed as a free digital toolkit for students. Instead of jumping between different websites for small calculations, study references, programming help and everyday utilities, StuPivot brings useful resources together in one place.

The goal is simple: make helpful digital tools easier to find, easier to understand and easier to use.
