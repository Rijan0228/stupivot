ABOUT STUPIVOT

Built to make student life simpler.

StuPivot is a free student-focused platform designed to bring academic calculators, study resources, programming learning and useful digital tools together.

The goal is simple: reduce the time students spend searching for small tools and help them focus more on learning, practicing and building.
