Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information may be processed when you use StuPivot, a student-focused website. It is a general template and should be reviewed and adapted to your actual services and local legal requirements.

1. Information you provide

If you send a message or feedback, the name, email address, rating and message you enter are sent to Formspree, the form-processing service configured for this website, so the site owner can receive your submission. Please do not submit passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information.

2. Browser storage and cookies

The site may use browser storage to remember your theme or privacy choice. These preferences are stored in your browser and are not used to identify you. Optional analytics should remain disabled unless you choose to accept it through the consent banner. If analytics is enabled in a future deployment, this policy should name the provider and explain its data practices.

3. How information is used

  • To read and respond to messages or feedback you submit.
  • To remember basic website preferences.
  • To understand site usage only if optional analytics is configured and you consent.
  • To protect the site and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Third-party services

Form submissions are processed by Formspree under its own policies. The site may load a third-party date-conversion library from jsDelivr. Those providers may process technical connection data when your browser requests their services.

5. Retention and security

Messages may remain in the form provider’s dashboard or email inbox until they are deleted. Reasonable steps should be taken to limit access, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Children and student information

StuPivot is intended for general student use. Avoid sharing personal information beyond what is needed to contact the site owner. If you believe you submitted information by mistake, contact the site owner and request deletion.

7. Your choices

You can reject optional analytics in the consent banner, clear browser storage through your browser settings, and choose not to submit a form. Browser storage preferences may be reset if you clear site data.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website changes. For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the contact form.
